FIRST-TIME SETUP

Get game-ready

Setup is mostly automatic. Finish these checks once.

01

Install and restart

MicDrop Setup installs VB-CABLE for you. Choose Restart Windows now when setup finishes so the cable devices become available.

02

Open MicDrop

MicDrop finds CABLE Input and starts the audio engine automatically. Your microphone begins muted on a new installation to prevent feedback.

03

Turn on your microphone

Under Audio routing, confirm your headset or USB microphone is selected, then click Mic off to change it to Mic on.

04

Configure the game

In Steam or the game’s voice settings, choose CABLE Output as the microphone. Use Borderless Windowed mode if the overlay is hidden by fullscreen.
